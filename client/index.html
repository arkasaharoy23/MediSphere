Security

Built to handle care data responsibly

Every layer of MediSphere is designed with role-based access and encryption in mind, not bolted on after.

Encrypted by design

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, from registration to medical records.

Role-based access

A pharmacy never sees lab reports, a lab never sees prescriptions. Access matches role.

Verified providers

Doctors and hospitals are manually verified by our admin team before going live.

Always-on monitoring
